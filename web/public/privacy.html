Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Crystal Boot is a free football forecasting game. It keeps only what it needs to run the game.

What we store

  • Your Pi identity: the user id Pi gives this app (it's specific to Crystal Boot) and your Pi username, from Pi sign-in.
  • Your picks: the percentages you gave for each match, when you made them, and the points they scored.
  • Your leagues: the private leagues you started or joined, and their names.
  • Payments: for anything you buy with Pi, the order, the amount, and the Pi payment and transaction ids, so we can prove the purchase and deliver it.
  • Sign-in sessions: a scrambled (hashed) copy of your session token, which expires after 30 days. Your phone keeps the token itself.

What we don't store

  • No email address, phone number, wallet address or real name.
  • No location, contacts or device details.
  • No ads, no ad trackers, no analytics cookies.

Who can see it

Your username and scores appear on leaderboards and in the private leagues you're in. Your individual picks are shown only to you. Once a match is settled, the app shows the crowd's average forecast for it, which doesn't identify anyone.

The app runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the service. Payments go through Pi Network's payment system. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Ask us through the Crystal Boot project on GitHub (jnbowmar/crystal-boot) and we'll delete your account, picks and league memberships. We may keep payment records where we need them for accounting.

Football data

Fixtures and results come from openfootball, which is public domain. The app shows team names only.
